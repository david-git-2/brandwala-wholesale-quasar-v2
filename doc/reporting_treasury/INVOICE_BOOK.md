# Invoice book report (report 3)

Sales ledger for a date range: issued invoices with billed, returned, cash, wallet, settlement, and still due per row.

**RPC:** `get_tenant_invoice_book_report`  
**Route:** `/:tenantSlug?/app/finance/reports/invoice-book`

Filters: date range, invoice type, payment status, search. Row click opens the existing invoice detail flow.
