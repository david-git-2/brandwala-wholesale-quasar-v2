# What is TradeFlow?

TradeFlow BD is **one company books** with several sell modules: wholesale, shop (catalog, fixed price, dropship), pre-order costing, and inbound warehouse. Parent tenant owns stock and cash; child brands and many shops plug into the same kernels.

Sell modules turn on per tenant. They share **money**, **warehouse**, **landed cost**, and **access** — so sales, cash, COD, and stock stay aligned across channels.
