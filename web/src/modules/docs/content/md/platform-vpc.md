## Labels

- **Value proposition**: TradeFlow BD platform
- **Customer segment**: Import / wholesale operator

## Customer jobs

- **Live catalogs**: Show buyers live catalogs, negotiated quotes, and new lines without maintaining parallel price lists in chat or spreadsheets.
- **Capture every order mode**: Take walk-in or trade bills, shop checkout, catalog negotiation, pre-order costing files, or dropship to a recipient.
- **Inbound and warehouse**: Track shipments, receive into warehouse lots, bin and grade stock, and fulfill picks.
- **Dropship operations**: Reseller buys at wholesale; the company ships to the end customer. Courier remittance and reseller profit stay separate from COD face.
- **Money and capital**: Customer bills and collections, supplier AP from landed shipments, cashbook leftover, and read-only investor visibility on shipment profit share.

## Customer pains

- **Overselling**: The same parent stock backs wholesale desk, shop carts, and dropship picks.
- **Quote chaos**: Custom and quote specs live in email while fulfillment runs in another tool.
- **Inbound blind spots**: Promising dates without knowing what is in transit, received, or still on a costing file.
- **Finance lies**: Treating COD as sales, mixing dropship merchant totals with recipient cash, or editing issued invoices instead of credit notes.
- **Stale catalogs**: Each shop or customer group needs different prices, visibility, or negotiation rules.

## Customer gains

- **One picture**: Open shipments, warehouse lots, shop orders, bills due, and cash in — with sales, cash, and COD defined the same way in reports.
- **No re-keying**: Sell standard, negotiated, pre-order, and dropship flows without copying SKU and party into separate systems.
- **Defensible cost**: Unit cost from inbound pays and locked shipment costs; gross profit from shipment P&L, not guessed invoice-line COGS.
- **Faster close**: Confirmed demand to pick, ship, bill, and collect — including Delivery paper close for pre-orders.
- **Self-serve shops**: Buyers use the right shop with the right prices and stock-backed availability.

## Products and services

- **Warehouse kernel**: Parent warehouse lots, bin tree, movements, FIFO issue on bills and shop picks.
- **Shop and catalogs**: Multiple shops per tenant; vendor catalog, fixed price, and dropship types; group pricing and listing visibility.
- **Order capture**: Wholesale compose (take / condition / walk-in); shop carts and negotiation; pre-order costing (PBC) and demand desk.
- **Inbound and landed cost**: Shipment lifecycle, cost buckets, AP bills per bucket × currency, lock and restamp from pays.
- **Money kernel**: Bills, pays, allocations, profile cashbook; dropship remittance and merchant leftover payout.
- **Access and investor**: Parent/child tenants, four login scopes (platform, app, shop, investor); investor portal read-only on shipment share.

## Pain relievers

- **One warehouse quantity**: Shop holds and pick lines debit the same lots; server-side issue and pick RPCs enforce availability — no channel-by-channel stock books.
- **Quote to demand**: PBC files and catalog negotiation lock confirmed qty into demand; Delivery paper close issues take or condition bills through the money kernel.
- **Shipment statuses**: Draft through received; post-to-stock from sellable lines; demand and backlog tie to profiles before goods are on hand.
- **Bills, pays, cashbook**: Merchant AR at ship, net remittance pay-in, leftover on profile cashbook; investor share on shipment investments, not a shadow ledger.
- **Per-shop listings**: Markup rules, group hides, and dropship floor prices; staff catalog browse separate from the customer shop.

## Gain creators

- **Locked money layers**: Reporting treasury reads sales vs cash vs COD vs AR; staff dashboard attention queue deep-links into desks.
- **Modules on one parent**: Child brands and new dropship or catalog shops without splitting warehouse or cashbook.
- **Shipment P&L**: Landed and local costs; portal shows partner share on batches — collections stay on staff money desks.
- **Receive to pick**: Inbound milestones feed lots and unit stamp; dropship processing picks against those lots then ship+issue in one step.
